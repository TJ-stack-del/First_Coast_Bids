Decided 2026-09-23: instead of a photo, the landing page packet shows real
miniatures of pages 2-4 of `sample-packet.pdf`, fanned behind the drawn
navy cover (`public/sample-packet/*.webp`, used by
`components/marketing/SampleSpecimen.tsx`). No printer or binder needed,
and what visitors see is the actual deliverable. If the packet builder's
layout changes, regenerate the PDF and re-render those pages
(`pdftoppm -r 110 -f 2 -l 4 -png sample-packet.pdf page`, then resize to
560px wide WebP). The printing and photo notes below are kept in case a
real photo is wanted later.

  a navy binding cover.
