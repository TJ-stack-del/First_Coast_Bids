# Sample packet for the landing page photo (2026-09-23)

Print these, bind them, and photograph the result to replace the drawn
packet cover on the landing page (`components/marketing/SampleSpecimen.tsx`).

- `sample-packet.pdf` (4 pages): generated by the real client packet builder
  (`lib/pdf/deliverables-packet.ts`, now in brand navy #0C2D52 and gold
  #C19349) from synthetic content: "Sample Clean Services (synthetic)",
  "Sample Agency (synthetic)", solicitation "SAMPLE-0000". Bracketed
  placeholders such as `[License number]` are deliberate: they show the
  never-invent-a-fact rule on the page.
- `cover-label.pdf`: a white label with crop marks, to cut out and attach to
  a navy binding cover.

## Shooting it (reference: the photo shared on 2026-09-23)

- Navy cover with the label, the three documents fanned out behind it so
  their headings show.
- Daylight from a window to one side; no flash or overhead light.
- Warm plain surface (a wood table), packet centred, some space around it.
- Portrait orientation, full phone resolution.

Nothing in the photo may show a real client's name, numbers or agency. The
landing page keeps its "Sample, synthetic" labelling.
