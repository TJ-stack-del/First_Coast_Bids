v0.3: changes from the critique
  • Packet is smaller and lies flat, with the three documents fanned behind the cover so it visibly holds three things. It now sits beside the "Every line traced" heading, so the hero leads straight into the proof.
  • One reading face: all running text is Archivo. The serif is only for headings, call-out titles, plan names and the packet caption.
  • Monospace only for real figures: page numbers, prices, solicitation numbers. Trades, status tags and "On us" moved out of it.
  • Gold appears once, as the packet's foil. Pilot is marked by a navy top rule and a light navy tint.
  • Placeholders never break across lines. On phones the numbered circles sit in a left margin instead of colliding with status tags.
  • Still open: plan button labels ("Start a pilot bid" etc.) are a copy change for you to approve.
